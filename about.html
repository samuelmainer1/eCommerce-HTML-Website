About Us

We are Big Drop

Leading online shopping store in Nairobi, Kenya — and your one-stop shop for e-commerce and order fulfilment. Reliability, speed and convenience through tech-driven, all-inclusive services from Globeflight Kenya.

Globeflight Kenya brings you Big Drop, a solution for online vendors to sell more. Do you have an online shop or sell online? Is storage and delivery getting too hectic? Focus on selling and let Big Drop pick, pack, store and deliver for you.

We give small traders who cannot afford a full e-commerce website the chance to sell online. With warehousing and logistics from Globeflight Kenya, we deliver to the end user efficiently — strong timelines for customers and hands-off fulfilment for vendors.

Businesses moving to e-commerce face warehousing, speedy delivery and packaging challenges. Globeflight Kenya is equipped and certified for the end-to-end process: ship, clear, warehouse, collect, e-fulfilment and deliver. With Big Drop E-Order Fulfilment, we are your one-stop shop.
