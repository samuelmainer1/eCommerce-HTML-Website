Returns & Refunds

Our return policy is designed to be fair for customers and sellers. Read the rules below before you request a return.

Return window

You may request a return within 7 days of delivery for eligible items that are unused, undamaged, and in original packaging with all accessories and tags.

What can be returned

  • Wrong item delivered
  • Damaged or defective product (report with photos where possible)
  • Item significantly not as described
  • Change of mind on eligible non-personal-care items (subject to inspection)

What cannot be returned

  • Opened personal care / hygiene products (e.g. some beauty items)
  • Perishable food items once delivered in good condition
  • Items damaged by misuse after delivery
  • Products without original packaging or missing parts

How to request a return

  1. Have your Order ID and tracking number ready.
  2. Contact us via Support or email orders@bigdrop.co.ke.
  3. Describe the issue and attach photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
  4. Our team will confirm if the return is approved and how to hand the parcel back.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed after the item is received and inspected. Refunds are typically returned to the original payment method or as agreed with our support team. M-Pesa refunds usually reflect within a few business days after approval.
