Delivery Info

BigDrop Kenya focuses on reliability and speed. We pick, pack and deliver so customers and vendors get a smooth experience.

Delivery timelines

  • Nairobi: often 1–3 business days
  • Major towns: typically 2–5 business days
  • Other areas: may take longer depending on courier routes

Timelines are estimates and can vary during peak seasons or for remote locations.

How delivery works

  1. Your client places an order on BigDrop.
  2. The order is received and processed in our fulfilment flow.
  3. Items are picked, packed and zoned for dispatch.
  4. A rider/courier contacts the customer before delivery.
  5. Delivery is confirmed and the system status is updated.

You can follow progress anytime on the Track Order page using your tracking number.

Delivery fees

A standard delivery fee may apply at checkout (e.g. KSh 200 in the demo). Selected orders may qualify for free delivery during promotions. Use promo code FREESHIP in the demo checkout where available.

Missed delivery

If you miss the rider’s call, contact +254 722 359 298 or orders@bigdrop.co.ke with your Order ID so we can rearrange delivery.
